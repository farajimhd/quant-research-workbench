# Causal participation ranking research

The scorer consumes completed multiresolution channels from v5. All numerical
weights, resolutions, minimum known coverage and row bounds are parameters.
It combines log relative volume and log relative trade activity with an upper
wick/range penalty. It retains per-resolution scores and availability counts.
Unknown channels remain null; partial coverage requires an explicit policy.
Labels and undeclared columns never reach the output. Future availability fails.

This is a pure research scorer. It grants no observation, entry, add, reduce,
exit, cash, fill or recovery permission. Certified admission and actual native
execution remain mandatory. Tie-breaking, opportunity-set completeness and
Portfolio cash allocation remain outside this projection.

The development comparison motivates testing participation plus wick as ranking,
not as a universal veto. The episode cohort is future-selected and correlated;
its descriptive AUC is not an executable reward, profitability or edge proof.
No native strategy consumes this scorer yet. Fundamental/split integration,
actual opportunity-stream comparisons and financial acceptance remain open.
