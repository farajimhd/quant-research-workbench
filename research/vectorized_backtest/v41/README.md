and before/after initialization RNG states are reused with only the version

V40 deterministic mutation allocations, rank-biased parent selection, isolated
per-offspring/operation/retry exploration streams and archive quantiles remain.

Added crossover mechanisms follow V36:
- 1/2/3 siblings per pair at 75/20/5 requested group quotas. Species budgets clip
  the final group; reciprocal siblings alternate primary parents.
- 40/30/30 inherit/exchange/compose action requests; 1..5 changed actions.
- Composition: tanh40%, min10%, max10%, product10%, gate20%, attention10%.
- 70% compatible module exchange, choosing deterministically among the four
  closest feature/temporal/shape/output signatures; 30% unmatched exchange.
- 25% exchange adapter requests, using bounded scale [0.25,4] and offset when
  at least eight valid nonconstant observations exist; otherwise explicit skip.
- 50% whole-action inheritance requests carry related policy genes; coupled
  swing parameters remain together. Ordinary one-field policy exchange remains.

Finite requested choices use deterministic interleaved quotas and rotating
locations, with novel numerical values/feature proposals remaining seeded.
Mating routes are scheduled at sibling-group boundaries: realized child-level
80/20 proportions can differ because siblings share a pair, clipping, invalid
proposals, ancestry restrictions and fresh fallbacks. Telemetry records both.

Descriptors use only a fixed training probe prefix (one window, eight clocks,
eight listings), float32 Numba without fastmath, and a bounded 256-network LRU.
They do not use session fitness or validation outcomes. Generation telemetry
adds requests/acceptance, adapters, linked policies, siblings and operator-tagged
parent improvement/elite survival from already evaluated training evidence.
Multi-action/mutated offspring can appear under several tags; attribution is
observational rather than a causal operator test. No extra financial replay.

The queue waits without Torch/CUDA for exact V40 controller/descendant release,
100 sealed local generations, final checkpoint/archive hashes and successful
exit. It then runs short CPU/GPU boundary and matched captured replay checks,
seeds only its own qualified compiler cache, and launches one fresh campaign.
Failures or owner changes fail closed. A single ordinary nonmodal detailed
Phase 1 terminal is retargeted only after V40 finishes. Memory admission retains
12 GiB reserve plus 8 GiB load admission. No long qualifications.
