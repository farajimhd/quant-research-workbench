What are “rule-implied label” and “rule comparison”?

Rule-implied label is the eligible or ineligible label suggested by the separate title-pattern rulebook. It is diagnostic context—not the V61 prediction.

Rule comparison explains whether that rulebook suggestion agrees with the gold label, disagrees, conflicts with another matched rule, or cannot produce one binary suggestion.
