# Current RL and optimization input review

[Completion audit](13i-completion-audit.md) · [Issuer collections](13r-issuer-cohorts.md)

This review covers common source inputs, without generating feature banks,
training shards, labels, optimizer exports or caches. It inspected current RL
V6 feature/execution definitions and the V18 optimizer enrichment reader/math.
The V18 area is under concurrent development; this is an October 13 snapshot,
not a frozen future consumer release.

## RL V6 input mapping

The current definitions contain 37 candle scalar names, 11 fields per V7 slot
(five below/five above), and 11 execution feature names.

| Consumer input group | Certified common authority | Required interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| OHLC, volume, trades, bar/session VWAP, price/extreme masks and observed-candle gaps | `research_bars_v1`; matching receipt and dated membership | Actual completed 1s bars; not padded seconds. VWAP derives from notional/volume with masks. Preserve integer price basis and actual UTC clocks. |
| EMA 7/26, MACD, RSI 14, ATR 14 and readiness | `research_indicators_v1`, `research_indicator_state_v1` | Same certified bar attempt/recipe and causal predecessor; readiness is not inferred from non-null numbers. |
| 60s activity and same-clock prior-session 10s RVOL | Certified current/prior bars and `research_activity_v1` | RVOL requires the preceding exchange session, not merely an elapsed 10s window. `context.plan` already declares this demand and seed preparation includes its session. Missing/zero prior denominator retains an unavailable mask. |
| Float, outstanding shares, split/reverse-split age and presence | `research_facts_v1`, certified float/supply and corporate-action scopes | Public-as-of clocks and matching security/share basis. Announcements are not execution confirmation; issuer totals are not automatically one security's shares. |
| V7 geometry, observations, age, roles, origin and presence | Causal `research_v7_checkpoint_v1` plus certified 1s bars | Select the preceding causal PM/regular/AH boundary and replay the same kernel as needed. Retrospective historical labels cannot supply intraday causal confirmation. No per-candle V7 tensor is generated by this campaign. |
| Spread, quote age/validity, size-conditioned fill and slippage inputs | `research_liquidity_v1` | Use completed 100ms evidence, quoted sizes, execution volume/notional and `quote_timestamp_us`; do not invent depth or treat a stale quote as fresh. Budget/fee/participation calculations remain consumer policy. |
| Time-of-day/session flags and gaps | UTC bar clocks, New York session/calendar contracts | Session boundaries include calendar/early-close semantics; preserve overnight gaps. |

The shared liquidity aggregate is produced independently of which optional bar
resolutions are published. The required 1000ms bar frame is already enforced
for causal V7. Higher consumer candle scales can be derived from certified 1s
primitives; their existence does not require another historical event fetch.

The current common metric catalog contains 106 metrics, including SEC/app
reported and derived metrics, float/outstanding-share variants, short volume,
short interest, price and corporate/allocation metrics. Catalog presence proves
schema/formula scope, not historical coverage or available publication evidence.

## Optimization context and rankings

V18 enrichment uses completed-clock own returns/volatility over 5/15/60/300s,
candle scales 5/15/60s, activity acceleration, fee-relative volatility,
cross-sectional return summaries and trailing-60s dollar-volume rankings.
These require common bars, validity masks, notional/volume, causal clocks and
the complete dated eligible population. The reader's declared endpoint-age
limit is one second; sparse/missing bars are unavailable, not zero returns.

Cross-sectional summaries/ranks must join **all certified collection members**
for the date before calculating population coverage or percentiles. Calculating
within each issuer cohort would change the denominator and produce a different
feature. Unavailable source units remain visible in coverage evidence. Partial
collection completion cannot authorize a full-universe ranking.

## Remaining acceptance boundary

Current RL V6 readers still query legacy `arte.bars_v1`/`indicators_v1`; optimizer
source adapters use legacy build IDs/runtime-ledger proofs. They do not yet
consume normalized research dataset IDs/receipts or collection roots. Common
reader binding/consumer parity must be addressed before claiming effortless
use of the new certified set. This review does not modify downstream code.

Historical identity, corporate/allocation/float evidence, short publication
clocks and SEC range gaps remain blockers even when all input columns exist.
No new required market primitive was identified in this review, but real
consumer-read parity and representative production coverage remain unproven.

## Shared bounded market reader

`pipelines.certified_research.market_reader.read` now reads bars, indicators,
100ms liquidity and rolling activity from a frozen `Scope`. It independently
verifies the full product receipt before and after the read, pins its attempt,
and rejects an absent/changed receipt or a row-bound overflow. Only completed
buckets with end time at or before the timezone-aware cutoff are returned.
`bucket_start_us` and `bucket_end_us` derive from New York midnight and the
stored resolution/index, retaining DST and exact microsecond cutoff semantics.
Price integers, readiness/validity masks and sparse gaps are preserved.

Callers must supply their claim and an `authority_guard` that verifies the
frozen dataset/collection, source and dated membership. A no-op guard is only
appropriate for isolated fixtures; a product receipt alone does not certify
full-universe completeness. Resolution selection is explicit (liquidity needs
`resolutions=(100,)`), and results are bounded to at most one million rows.
No default current-ticker join, padding or cross-sectional ranking is performed.
This is a common-data read primitive, not migration of the existing RL/optimizer
adapters or proof of their parity. Missing historical authority still blocks
production use.
