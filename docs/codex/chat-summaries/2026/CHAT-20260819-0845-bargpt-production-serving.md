### Narrative

The user initially asked for an evidence-led production design for the trained BarGPT v2 and v3 models, starting from a drafted `market-ai` service and using QMD Live plus QMD History or the database for context. The requirements quickly became concrete: the service had to respect Live, Paper, Replay, Backtest, and Backtest Debug clocks; warm a configurable liquid Watchlist; admit and warm new members during the session; maintain only the bounded context each model needs; support Auto and Manual triggering; and use vectorized, bounded concurrent work. Model heads had to become ordinary application Data Fields that could feed Rule Sets, Signal Streams, Market Discovery, and chart indicators rather than form a second decision system.

Review found that `services/market-ai` was not a viable shortcut. It used byte-window smoke encoders and placeholder temporal models, while BarGPT requires the certified sparse multiview inputs, masks, origin and as-of indices, calendar context, target clocks, and target support encoded by the versioned research loaders. The stale generic service was therefore removed. Its still-valid contextual-news responsibility was preserved separately as `services/news-hypothesis`. A new `services/bar-gpt` became the only BarGPT inference boundary and reused the v2/v3 loaders and saved checkpoints directly. No model architecture change or retraining was required merely to serve the checkpoints.

The discussion clarified autoregressive semantics. Serving performs one non-recursive forward pass when a completed origin becomes available, publishes every model head, then incorporates the next actual bar. Causal attention is correct for this use. Incremental transformer KV reuse was rejected for the initial authority because the checkpoints do not expose a certified cache contract across every ticker, model, view, decoder layer, attention-window eviction, padding mask, irregular bar completion, session boundary, split-normalization change, correction, and multiview as-of relationship. Full-prefix inference remains authoritative; dynamic cross-ticker batching is the safe first optimization. Any KV implementation must prove numerical parity against full recomputation before activation.

The service was built with fixed-length per-ticker causal caches, QMD History bootstrap, QMD compact-event streaming, bounded warm concurrency, bounded inference queues, dynamic batching, watchlist-driven scopes, late-correction handling, prediction journals, and separate cache authorities for Live/Paper and each historical run. Historical future rows stay outside the active cache until the replay clock makes them available. Manual mode keeps context current without inference; Auto mode schedules inference at completed eligible origins. New Watchlist members cannot infer until warm-up succeeds, and removed members are reclaimed when no scope owns them.

An early historical path updated model scopes asynchronously. Tracing showed that a fast Replay or Backtest could evaluate a BarGPT-dependent Rule Set before GPU publication arrived. The design was corrected: when the frozen configuration references `model.bargpt.*`, the historical runtime uses a synchronous, fail-closed inference barrier at the causal strategy frame. Historical runs without model dependencies retain the cheaper asynchronous scope update. Live/Paper prediction availability is post-inference wall-clock availability; historical predictions are admitted only at the logical causal clock enforced by the barrier.

QMD Live gained a ticker-filtered compact-event batch stream with bounded event count, delay, sequence evidence, and existing lag/resnapshot semantics. The backend gained BarGPT health, scope, inference, prediction, and model-feature APIs plus a mode/scope/ticker/model store that prevents historical forecasts from leaking into Live. The application registry exposed 2,984 unique v2/v3 fields, including every original checkpoint output and separate decoded values or probabilities. Generic labels such as “Vector,” “Logits,” or “Probability” were replaced with model-, product-, horizon/view-, head-, quantile-, and representation-specific names.

Output presentation was revised after the user clarified that forecasts are bars. Canvas gained selectable forecast candles plus independent open, high, low, and close lines. Users can choose v2 or v3, q10/q50/q90, one horizon or all horizons, and Auto or Manual triggering. Forecast candles are translucent future OHLC bars and render only when geometry is valid; invalid OHLC is not silently repaired. The line components remain independently selectable. `q50` is the model’s median quantile forecast, while q10 and q90 are lower and upper quantiles, not derivative fields. The unmodified raw model heads remain available alongside decoded chart fields.

Operational ownership was split deliberately. Market Discovery owns revisioned application intent: Watchlists, enabled model use, maximum ticker count, trigger mode, Data Fields, Rule Sets, and Signal Streams. Services → BarGPT owns immutable release admission, champion/shadow roles, checkpoint and contract hashes, effective versus desired restart-required settings, device/precision, batching, queues, cache readiness, scopes, retention, and QMD connectivity. Arbitrary checkpoint paths are never browser-editable or exposed in service payloads. The missing `service-bar-gpt` route was fixed and the operational page was implemented using existing application patterns. Commits `5b9e06e2`, `faa6366f`, and `f3ab678b` delivered the initial service, historical barrier, and operational UI.

### Durable decisions

- **Confirmed requirements:** all runtime modes use their own causal clocks; dynamic Watchlists warm before inference; Auto and Manual triggers share the same continuously updated context; outputs integrate through existing Data Fields, Rule Sets, Signal Streams, Market Discovery, and Canvas.
- **Architectural decisions:** QMD/QMD History and canonical ClickHouse data remain source authorities; BarGPT owns model-ready context and predictions only. Historical runs are cache-isolated. BarGPT-dependent strategy frames use a synchronous fail-closed barrier.
- **Release decisions:** only immutable checkpoint hashes plus contract hashes and evaluation evidence may be admitted. V2 remains champion and v3 shadow. Loading successfully is not promotion evidence.
- **Output decisions:** preserve every raw head; decoded quantiles and forecast prices are separate fields. q50 means median, q10/q90 lower/upper quantiles. Invalid forecast-bar geometry is exposed rather than repaired.
- **Performance decisions:** full-prefix inference is authoritative. Cross-ticker batching is enabled; incremental KV reuse remains prohibited until parity is certified.
- **Ownership decisions:** operational configuration belongs under Services; revisioned model-use intent belongs under Market Discovery. The browser never selects arbitrary checkpoint paths.
- **Rejected approaches:** the stale Market AI smoke model, recursive forecast generation, filename-recency promotion, cross-mode cache sharing, silent incomplete-history fallback, silent prediction skipping, and naïve KV-token eviction.
- **Later clarification:** TASK-0217 subsequently changed the paced target from 500 to 100 symbols once per second and stopped evaluation on 2026-09-29. This archival summary does not supersede that later evidence or authorize resumption.
