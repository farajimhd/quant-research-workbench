### Narrative

The chat began after the live News Intelligence deployment exposed obviously wrong DeepFM forecast-eligibility decisions. The user highlighted two decisive error types: multi-ticker mover roundups such as “12 Industrials Stocks Moving In Tuesday's After-Market Session,” and delayed price-reaction follow-ups such as “YY Group Stock Jumps 90% After Hours: Here's Why” arriving after the actual issuer announcement. The user’s objective evolved into two linked programs: correct suspicious 2025-2026 gold labels for retraining, and improve deterministic News Synthesis so title patterns, metadata, provider context, and event semantics agree with the reviewed policy. Forecast eligibility, sentiment, reaction, and direction remained separate concepts.

The first diagnosis disproved the initial assumption that the mover-roundup error came from training labels. All 3,875 matching 2025-2026 mover templates and all 72 matching August-holdout rows were already gold-ineligible, and the evaluated DeepFM gave them probabilities below 0.001. The deployed release instead produced probability 1.0. Reloading the promoted checkpoint reproduced the production error despite identical structured inputs. The root cause was serving transform drift: training stored inverse maximum-absolute column multipliers and multiplied by them, while serving interpreted the artifact as a divisor. Hash and dimension checks passed because they did not validate transform semantics or replay predictions.

Commit `ea36d59a` repaired the fundamental serving contract. `deepfm_serving.py` now calls the shared multiplier function, the serving contract records `operation=multiply` and inverse-max-absolute semantics, and promotion replays the complete 4,913-row frozen August population in a fresh process. Promotion also validates batched CPU output, batch-size-one canaries including mover templates, and GPU parity when available. The repaired release is `news-deepfm-pre-august-v1-scaling-repair-v2`. Commits `225d65bb` and `1c452b79` added a restart-safe correction ledger and repair runner. The runtime repair processed all 2,030 rows created by the broken release: 2,016 were rescored, 14 unavailable sources failed closed, and 1,103 changed from eligible to ineligible. Historical signal occurrences were preserved as provenance rather than silently rewritten. The report is under `D:\TradingML\runtimes\text_intelligence\serving\news_forecast_funnel_v1\scaling_repair_v2`.

### Durable decisions

- **Confirmed requirements:** multi-ticker mover lists, why-moving/price-reaction follow-ups, earnings results/recaps, routine analyst forecasts, routine holdings, and the other reviewed context families are forecast-ineligible. Transcripts are eligible and must be synthesized from transcript content. Mixed event families require row evidence or explicit precedence rather than blanket flips.
- **Authority precedence:** immutable operator/manual decisions outrank conflicting subagent votes; resolved nonconflicting blind re-audits may update the parent; unresolved non-operator rows are excluded; unaudited parent rows must remain exact. Feature or title patterns discover candidates but never silently authorize gold changes.
- **Evaluation boundary:** training/development authority and August holdout remain separate. The exposed August holdout must not be tuned again. A later sealed, prediction-blind time-forward holdout is required before certifying a new DeepFM release or threshold.
- **Serving contract:** column-transform meaning is versioned and replayed, not inferred from a filename. Promotion requires fresh-process full-population replay, batch-size-one canaries, CPU/batch parity, and GPU parity when available.
- **Production boundary:** DeepFM remains the sole live forecast-eligibility authority at threshold 0.5; News Synthesis does not silently replace live routing. Gold correction and model retraining are separate operations.
- **Rejected approaches:** ticker/article hardcoding, automatic pattern-based flips without review, treating hash/dimension validation as behavioral parity, rewriting downstream provenance, allowing the assignment CSV to replace a corrected parent, and tuning on the already exposed August holdout.
