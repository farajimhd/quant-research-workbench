## User objectives and intended optimization shape — preserve first

The user repeatedly emphasized these requirements. Later clarifications supersede earlier versions; aspirations are distinguished from delivered behavior below.

- **Learn trading from causal market data.** Candidates should learn entry, exit, add, reduce, stop and target behavior. Execution must not interpret a swing identifier or invent trading decisions for a candidate: strategy programs consume available market references and emit explicit prices. Order accounting must be realistic, minimal and inspectable.
- **Grow useful networks bottom up.** The intended initial networks contain only 1–2 nodes per action, approximately 5–10 total, with mutation adding useful nodes/edges and crossover combining subgraphs or complete strategies. Do not silently initialize large networks or reduce evolved growth limits to reach speed targets. The evolved total/per-action ceiling remains 1,024 under the construction contract.
- **Stateless, batched inference.** Remove recurrent edges, previous-action/fill feedback and ultimately all position feedback from neural inputs. Each session's inference is independent of chronological execution. Execution may retain inventory/accounting and strategy-side request guards, without feeding those states into the neural network. Removing neural recurrence does not remove chronological accounting.
- **One creation authority.** Sampling, crossover, mutation and routing must use the same feature/history/parameter authority, including joint relations rather than independent scalar bounds. Forbidden inputs must be absent from the final tensor. Clamp construction parameters to agreed feasible bounds where appropriate; replace genuinely rejected candidates so every batch is full. Do not let invalid proposals repeatedly consume replay budget.
- **Compact attention and data.** Inference uses the current top five tickers and only the population-required feature union, in time blocks. Derive swing/candle variations in memory from existing shards, with no added shard data, download or database query. Execution loads top-five obligations plus held/pending tickers needed during ranking changes. Rank-loss exits remain executable after a ticker leaves inference attention.
- **No hidden trading restrictions or silent accounting fixes.** Remove the historical $300 risk and 20% stop-distance caps from selection and execution. Initial stops must be below entry; trailing stops may protect profit above entry. Wait three seconds after a full exit before re-entry. Use quote sizes and whole shares; fixed $1,000 initial entries and $200 adds, with at most five filled add orders per position, counted on first fill and reset after closing. Neural outputs cannot change these budgets. Request limits belong in the strategy adapter before submission.
- **Score broad usefulness, not a single lucky day.** The user repeatedly challenged candidates profitable on one session and inactive/loss-making elsewhere. Validation is display-only. Inspect the same candidate across all sessions; do not confuse a table's maximum score and maximum net with one individual. Preserve explicit score components and penalties.

Parent draws include 25% ticker specialists / 75% global-ranked selection. Blind reproduction should mix multiple/all session origins, with lineage and technique performance visible. Accepted strategy-level alternatives included entry routing, reciprocal veto and entry–management block exchange. These historical immigration requirements do not override the later fresh global-only campaign.

Drawdown remains diagnostic; there is no separate gross-loss subtraction. Validation never selects parents or calibrates founders.

Final fixed order budgets and add limits take precedence over historical sizing genes.
