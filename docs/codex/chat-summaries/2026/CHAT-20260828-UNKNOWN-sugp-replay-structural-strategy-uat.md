Exit and re-entry policy evolved through chart review. Protective exits below entry must take the first of bearish structural change, stop loss, forming one-second MACD closure, or price loss of executable VWAP. Above entry, the strategy should remain in the campaign until MACD closes, except for a structural limit target intended only to catch a sudden upward wick. Intermediate profit-taking ladders and a fixed re-entry count were rejected. The accepted target selection uses qualified structural resistance levels with hold probability at least 85%; as each one-second candle closes above a qualified level, the target advances causally to a higher ordinal. The final UAT used the third qualified level. If a target is touched but not fully filled, all remaining entry orders must be cancelled and the rest of the position exited immediately. After an exit or target, re-entry remains eligible while the completed-candle MACD regime is positive/open and the current entry structure passes. `maximum_reentries_reached`, the stale 250-basis-point-above-VWAP rule, ambiguous `trim` wording, and the legacy non-executable VWAP authority were rejected.

### Durable decisions

- **Discovery versus management:** `Early Squeeze Move` admits a ticker once. It is not a recurring entry or re-entry gate.
- **VWAP authority:** executable VWAP is the sole current VWAP authority for chart and strategy. The legacy observation VWAP must not drive current behavior.
- **Entry clock:** entry uses a completed one-second MACD candle, requires line greater than signal and both greater than zero, and may apply a small explicit noise threshold. Other market, quote, liquidity, and structural facts remain event-driven.
- **Exit clock:** exits use the forming one-second MACD and act as soon as signal exceeds line. Below-entry protection also honors bearish structure, stop, and VWAP loss, whichever occurs first.
- **Structure:** entry levels require hold at least 80% and maximum breaks 100. Targets require hold at least 85% and are advanced only after a completed one-second close accepts a level.
- **Target purpose:** the target catches sudden wicks; it is not routine profit-taking. The current experiment uses one target at the third qualified level. The proposed 50/50 second/third-level split was explicitly rejected.
- **Re-entry:** no arbitrary maximum re-entry count. Re-entry remains possible in the same admitted campaign when the completed MACD regime and current structural frontier pass.
- **Lifecycle authority:** one position contains many orders and one order contains many fills. Position quantity is integer and closes only when aggregate open quantity becomes zero.
- **No hardcoding:** SUGP is a bounded UAT ticker, never a source of ticker-specific rules or constants.
- **Acceleration boundary:** order-free events and historical structure acquisition may be batched or prefetched, but active-order matching remains causally ordered and canonical state is refreshed after each fill event.
- **Historical journal durability:** reproducible Replay and Backtest journals may use SQLite WAL `NORMAL`; paper/live journals retain `FULL` durability.
- **Testing order:** do not backtest all tickers until the full SUGP session is visually and causally accepted. Only then rebuild every ticker's level book and expand to two premarket sessions.
