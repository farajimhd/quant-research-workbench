### Narrative

This chat began with a forensic review of `N1130/SNCY`. The user challenged two outcomes: News Synthesis had classified a company announcement reported through a news service as analyst-origin, and the manual gold sentiment was mixed even though the adverse transaction evidence appeared dominant. That case established the governing standard for the rest of the work. The engine could not be repaired through a ticker, article ID, or one-off phrase; gold labels had to reflect the overall economic balance rather than the mere presence of evidence on both sides; and every accepted code change needed an identical-population before/after audit proving that it fixed a general defect without introducing regressions.

The review expanded across the original 1,045-label authority. Individual packets exposed recurring failures in analyst attribution, forecast eligibility, regulatory and clinical language, contract termination, outlook tables, guidance comparisons, reverse splits, multi-ticker role attribution, financing, loss narrowing, and whole-event dominance. Examples included an FDA response interpreted as favorable when it preserved an adverse blocker, a reverse split treated as positive, seller and buyer economics transferred between tickers, and coordinated earnings or outlook values flattened into unrelated sentences. The user repeatedly asked whether each failure admitted a fundamental repair and authorized implementation only when the answer was yes. Manual-gold errors were corrected separately from engine errors; articles such as `N0702/HIBB` and `N1064/A` reinforced that forecast eligibility depends on source origin, issuer role, currentness, and material evidence, not simply on a company name or financial number appearing in the article.

The engineering process matured into explicit audit loops. Each loop evaluated a frozen population, wrote mismatch-only Markdown packets under the runtime root, reviewed the packets, traced decisions to code, consolidated root-cause families, applied coherent generic repairs, and reran the same population. Missing predictions were treated as defects requiring a failure-stage explanation rather than silently dropped records. Broad fixes were rejected when their regression profile showed that they restored a desired label through document-wide leakage. In particular, blanket single-subject continuity, global alias fallback, and global mixed/dominance threshold changes could make one packet correct while contaminating unrelated issuers. The accepted direction was local identity authority, bounded section continuity, semantic transaction roles, structured event state, and issuer-local aggregation.

The remaining 955 labels were then converted into News Synthesis documents and manually reviewed, using narrow packet assignments so reviewers received only their news, metadata, and relevant certification contracts. This completed the 2,000-article manual authority. The first new-945 evaluation contained 298 eligible articles and 490 issuer units, with 253 exact sentiment matches, 237 mismatches, seven missing predictions, and zero engine failures. The seven missing units traced to an offline benchmark identity gap: genuinely absent source candidates lost reviewed identity-only fields. The repair allowed reviewed identity evidence only to restore an absent benchmark identity; it did not expose reviewed sentiment or eligibility to prediction and did not overwrite existing source identity.

Review of all 234 post-identity mismatch packets classified 198 gold labels as supportable, 18 as clearly wrong, and 18 as policy-uncertain. After explicit user approval, the 18 confirmed bad labels were corrected through reviewed specifications and the authoritative recertification path. The uncertain labels remained separate rather than being forced toward the engine. Generic polarity repairs addressed Earnings ESP values confused with price movements, composed analyst negation, minimum-bid deficiencies, executive-death versus interim-management offsets, and explicit absence of expected cost savings. Commit `b30d7b48` (`fix: repair news synthesis identity and polarity gaps`) preserved those changes. The final new-945 audit improved from 253 to 264 exact units, reduced mismatches from 237 to 226, eliminated all seven missing predictions, introduced no new errors, and had zero engine failures. The corresponding full-2,000 regression improved 13 units with no introduced errors and reduced missing predictions from 15 to eight.

### Durable decisions

- Confirmed requirement: production behavior must never branch on sample IDs, tickers, company names, or memorized article phrases. Repairs must model general identity, language, time, role, lifecycle, numeric, source, or aggregation behavior.
- Confirmed requirement: gold correction and engine correction are distinct authority operations. Gold changes require source review, reviewed-specification updates, validation, recertification, and a repeated frozen audit.
- Confirmed requirement: missing predictions, rejected units, engine failures, and identity-unscorable records must remain explicit and counted.
- Architectural decision: evaluation targets are scoring provenance, not provider identity evidence. Gold tickers must not change ordinary source resolution merely because they are evaluated.
- Architectural decision: issuer-local identity, source lane, semantic role, event state, and bounded section continuity outrank document-wide alias or single-subject propagation.
- Architectural decision: `news_synthesis_engine_v44` is the code version; V55 is an audit-run version generated by V44. Audit and engine numbers are not interchangeable.
- Architectural decision: forecast-trigger performance must report TP/FN/FP/TN, precision, recall, F1, specificity, and balanced accuracy. Raw accuracy alone is misleading on the imbalanced population.
- Architectural decision: article-grouped audit, development, and sealed-test populations prevent cross-ticker leakage from the same article. Test examples cannot be used to design repairs.
- Architectural decision: subagents should operate as a bounded reusable pool over small complete packet batches. They return structured results and do not edit authority or source concurrently.
- Rejected approach: optimize until zero errors. Iteration stops when no coherent high-confidence repair has a favorable regression profile; ambiguous or idiosyncratic cases remain explicit.
- Rejected approach: treat every company announcement as forecast-eligible. Eligibility still requires issuer-local, substantive, current or forward, non-analyst evidence with a material forecast-relevant implication.
- Unresolved uncertainty: the dedicated V44 2,000-authority eligibility audit had not yet been persisted when this chat handed off, and the remaining FP/FN gold decisions had not been manually adjudicated here.
