## Durable decisions

- Confirmed requirements: preserve assumed fills, exact accounting, canonical/causal input checks, price-filter-before-top10 funnel, five parent-link-generation restrictions, fresh random initialization and no past-result imports.
- Independent global cost, minimized: `-P_all + 0.25*H - 0.10*N*T20 + 0.05*sum(D_s) + 0.005*sum(R_s) + 0.0004*sum(E_s)`. `H=sum(max(P_s,0)^2)/sum(max(P_s,0))`, zero denominator gives zero; `T20` is the fractional mean of the worst 20% session profits, including inactive zeros. N is training session count.
- Independent local cost: `-P_s + 0.05*D_s + 0.005*R_s + 0.0004*E_s`. D is drawdown dollars; R and E are risk/capital dollar-hours. No complexity, shortfall, profit-trimming or worst-trade penalty in V16.
- Global 512 slots: 51 elites, 52 fresh immigrants, 409 tournament-selected crossovers. Local 512: up to 30 unique champions, up to 150 champion crossovers, remaining intrasession offspring and 102 fresh immigrants. Duplicate globally retained champions release local slots for offspring. Local pools have 13 candidates/session; bounded mate failures become counted immigrants without weakening ancestry rules.
- Mutation follows both reproduction paths; preserve the accepted rate/K/exclusions. The visible G-prefix inherits the first parent's origin and is not rewritten by mutation/crossover; separate metadata records actual creation generation.
- Validation shortlist is 512 unique candidates selected only from training, half global leaders and half session-balanced leaders. Freeze successor and training RNG before validation; validation cannot control automatic search decisions.
- Cross-day indicators, masked warmup, frozen listing identity and same-device exact parity are requirements. Rejected: daily reset, fabricated predecessor history, raw flatfile fallback, tolerance weakening and truncation.
- Architecture: separate nonblocking terminal and W&B processes. No production profitability claim; no full-generation speed claim from a synthetic fixture. No monitor scheduler restart without user direction.
